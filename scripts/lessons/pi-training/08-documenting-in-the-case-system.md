- **All information in the Intake and Case Summary tabs should be completed.** Intake gets as much information as it can, but it is the legal assistant's responsibility to confirm information and obtain what is missing. The welcome call is for confirming information and getting what is missing, for example: legal name, marital status, date of birth, Social Security number, copy of ID, copy of the front and back of the health insurance card, spouse information, emergency contact, previous accidents or claims, injured body parts, bankruptcy, child support and employment status.
- **Contact cards should always be completed.** Full legal name, date of birth, driver's license number if you have it (usually on the police report), phone number, email, fax, mailing address and so on, for **all clients, adjusters, defendants and providers**.
- **Most recent attempt and most recent contact** must be updated every time we attempt, or have a successful contact with, the client. If the client does not answer, send a text message and a follow-up email.

### Insurance tab

The insurance tab should **always have at least three rows**. There are statutes of limitations on filing claims, so if they are not filed on time, coverage could be denied.

1. Insurance tab: **Liability**
2. **UM insurance**
3. **Health insurance**
4. If the client is a passenger, we need **their personal insurance**, since it is primary.
5. If there is a **resident relative**, add the resident relative's name, relationship to the client, policy number and the name of the insurance company.

**Rules to remember**
- **Personal car insurance is primary.** If a client is a passenger in a vehicle, that vehicle's UM is secondary. **UM follows the person.**
- **Med pay follows the car, not the person.**
- Ask clients whether they have a resident relative, which could help increase the value of the case if the relative has UM.
- Add the health insurance information. If there is no health insurance, add a note saying so. Save a copy of the front and back of the health insurance card and a copy of the LOR. Follow up and get the health insurance EOB.
- If the client is a passenger or a driver, specify it next to the client's name in the project.
- Enter the date the LOR was mailed by certified mail and attach a copy.
- When a dec page is received, name it, save it and document the policy limits. Once the liability and UM dec pages are received and the limits are confirmed by the case manager, the phase can be changed.
- If there is **no liability insurance**, add a note, for example "Hit and Run". If coverage was **denied**, add a note and attach the coverage denial letter. Add **#liabilitydenied**, **#coverage-denied** or **#UMonly**.
- If there is **med pay**, enter the amount and a note of how much med pay coverage there is. Add **#medpay**.
- Once you have the police report, update the insurance tab with the defendant and insurance information (name, address, policy number, name of the insured, adjuster information, claim number and so on).

### Meds tab

All providers need to be listed.

- **Provider name.** If you do not know the provider's name, enter "Unknown chiro" and a note explaining why. Also set a reminder to follow up with the client and get the information.
- **Treatment status:** Actively treating or Treatment complete.
- **Notes:** enter information such as the current balance or any important note. The most recent note goes at the top. Format: **date, note, initials.** *Example: 5/1/20xx, called the liability adjuster, spoke with the adjuster, who gave verbal liability limits of 25/50. (initials)*
- Add lost wages notes and save a copy of the lost wages form.
- When medical records and bills are **requested**, enter the date of the request and add a copy of the request.
- When they are **received**, add the date received and name them.
- When the **final** medical records and bills are received, enter the dates of treatment and the medical totals. **Do not enter the totals if the client is still treating and we do not have the final bill.**
- Update the treatment status.

**If the client goes to the hospital**, we request the hospital bill, the ER physician bill and the radiologist bill (if applicable). List the provider as "Unknown ER Phys" as a reminder so you do not forget.
- The hospital bills and records must be requested **once we confirm coverage**, or once we can confirm our client has UM.
- If the client was treated at the ER more than once, add each visit separately and specify the dates of service, to be sure we request both ER physician bills, both ER hospital bills and both radiologist bills. Do not add them as one: they have different dates of service, charges and account numbers, and possibly different hospitals.
- Add **#hospital**.

**Pre-settlement loans.** Any pre-settlement loan should be saved in the medicals, but **do not enter the total amount** since it is not a medical bill. Note the loan amount in the notes section and save a copy of the loan documents. Add **#loan**.
