### Naming documents

Everything the firm receives must be **named in the case management system and in the shared document folders**. The items in the shared folders are named the same way as they are saved in the case management system.

Every description should include the **client's last name(s), first name and middle name**.

**Examples**
- State Farm liability dec page - (client's last names, first name, middle name)
- AICA medical records and $999 bill - (client's name)
- PP-CIOX $149.50 Northside Hosp records - (client's name)
- Signed Completed Exit Packet - (client's name)
- Signed WC contract - (client's name)
- Final Disposition - (client's name)

### Personal injury folders (subfolders of the case folder)

| Folder | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| **Onboarding** | Contract, ID, front and back of the health insurance card, HIPAA authorization, and any information obtained during onboarding. |
| **Correspondence** | Any correspondence received about the case: letters of acknowledgement, health insurance correspondence and questionnaire, case update requests from adjusters. |
| **Ins. Dec pages** | The liability and UM dec pages, including the signed UM selection form. |
| **Medical Records / Bills** | All medical records and itemized bills for the case. |
| **Lost Wages** | The lost wages form and the tax document that supports lost wages if the client is self-employed. |
| **Evidence** | Videos, pictures, the police report, or any other evidence for the case. |
| **Expenses** | Any expense related to the case, for example the police report, intake fee, postage, and invoices for medical records and bills. |
| **Demand** | A copy of the entire demand: liability, UM and med pay. |
| **Reductions** | All reduction requests and reduction approvals. |
| **Exit** | All exit packets and the documents that close the case. |

### Miscellaneous folder: criminal and workers' compensation cases

**Criminal subfolders**
- **Onboarding:** contract, ID, police report.
- **Evidence:** evidence related to the case.
- **Court Documents:** all court documents.
- **Exit:** all documents confirming the case is closed, and the final disposition form.

**Workers' compensation subfolders**
- **Onboarding:** the contract with the other attorney, and any documents the client provided during intake.
- **Exit:** the settlement check, once we receive it from the attorney.
