### Overview

This procedure explains how to archive a case when the firm decides to withdraw, or when the client terminates the firm, on a personal injury case. Every step below is required so the case is archived properly once we no longer represent the client. It applies only to cases the firm withdraws from or is terminated on.

**When a client terminates the firm, the withdrawal letters must be sent out within 48 hours of receiving the written termination.**

### Steps

1. **Add the tag** **#terminated** or **#withdrew** in the project vitals.
2. **Case Summary.** Go to Case Summary under the filter menu and scroll to the bottom. Under **Case Withdrawal**, choose the correct option to generate the matching questions. If the firm withdrew, click "Yes" and enter the reason, enter the withdrawal date and save a copy of the withdrawal letters in the Exit bucket. Choosing a template generates the withdrawal letter. Review that the information on the letter is correct before sending it to the client.
3. **Send the letters to the client**, with copies of the withdrawal letters sent to the insurance companies and all providers. The letter to the client is sent by **regular and certified mail**. Save the certified tracking number, and the copy of the file sent to the client, in the Exit bucket.
4. **If the firm is withdrawing, call the client** to tell them verbally. Confirm the client's name and mailing address, and give them the **statute of limitations**. Confirm we will be sending the withdrawal packet to the correct address. Giving the statute of limitations lets the client know their options if they want a second opinion.
5. **Send withdrawal letters** to all insurance companies, medical providers, funding companies, the health insurance, and anyone who received a letter of representation from us. These letters do not need to go by certified mail. In some cases, such as providers we work with or adjusters, you can email or fax the letter. Save a copy of all letters and notes in the Exit bucket.
6. **Check the records portals** (the records vendors' portals) to make sure there are no pending invoices. If there are, send a message to cancel them if possible. If not, we pay the pending invoices and take it as a loss.
7. **Email the operations director and the PI supervisor**, copying the file, about why we no longer represent the client. If the firm withdrew, state the reason. If the firm was terminated, state the reason in the email, for example "Hired another attorney" or "Unknown".
8. **Update the phase tracker** with the date the case was archived and the name of the person archiving it.
9. **Change the case phase to Archive.**

**Note:** Before withdrawing from a case, check with the attorney or supervisor and get approval. Have any documentation available for review and withdrawal approval. If the reason for withdrawal is no coverage, have the no-coverage letters in writing, including the signed UM rejection form if needed.
