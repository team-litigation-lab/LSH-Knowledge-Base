### Getting an invoice paid

On the invoice, **highlight the important information** so it is easy for accounting, or anyone, to read:
- patient / client name
- records from
- balance due
- invoice number
- who the check should be made payable to

Then:
1. Add a tab to the invoice with your initials, so accounting knows who to return the invoice and the check to.
2. Place the invoice in accounting's office, in the box where invoices go.
3. Email accounting a copy of the invoice and copy the file.

### Save the invoice

- Scan the invoice and upload it in the billable expenses.
- Also save a copy under the specific provider the invoice is for. *Example: an invoice for records from a hospital is saved in the Meds section under that hospital.*
- Name it in the case system and the shared folder as: **Invoice, provider name (for example the records vendor), $amount due, provider name (for example the hospital), client's name.**

### Document it in Billable Expenses

- **Type of expense:** Medical Records (this includes bills, since there is no drop-down for bills).
- **Form of payment:** Check (accounting gives you the check).
- **Amount:** the dollar amount due on the invoice.
- **Recipient:** the provider the check will be sent to, for example the records vendor.
- **Notes:** for example "Received invoice from (vendor). Scanned and saved in file. Emailed and put in accounting's box." Enter the invoice number and any special notes. Also document it in the Activity tab under Expenses.

### When accounting issues the check

Scan a copy of the check and of the invoice, and complete the documentation in the Billable Expense. Enter:
- check number
- date written
- date mailed
- mailed by

In the notes, include the address where you mailed it.
