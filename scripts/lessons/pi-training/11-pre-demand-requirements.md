**If any of the following is not complete or is missing, the demand is returned to the legal assistant until it is ready for pre-demand.**

### Checklist

- [ ] A copy of the client's ID has been saved to the file and the contact card.
- [ ] The UM dec page is saved and the limits are noted in the insurance tab. The adjuster information is updated on the contact card.
- [ ] The UM signed selection form is saved in the file.
- [ ] The liability dec page is saved and the limits are documented in the insurance tab.
- [ ] The current adjuster contact information has been confirmed by calling the adjuster. The contact card must be updated so the demand is delivered to the correct adjuster and address.
- [ ] Med pay has been exhausted, or the bills have been submitted to the med pay adjuster. The EOB has been requested. If there is no med pay, there is a note saying so, and a **#mpc** or **#nompc** tag. All med pay information is noted in the med pay bucket, including med pay checks and invoices.
- [ ] The health insurance subrogation log is saved in the file, and the reference number is noted in the insurance section. If the client has no health insurance, there is a note saying so in the health insurance section and in the health insurance bucket.
- [ ] All case expenses are noted in the Billable Expenses tab and in the activity section. Receipts are attached and named correctly in the file. **This includes checking the records portals (MRO, Chartswap and the records vendor portals), and adding the admin fee and any sign-up fee.** Save screenshots of the portals you checked, to confirm there are no pending unpaid invoices.
  - *Example:* PP $amount paid, provider name and what it is for, client's name.
  - *Example:* Invoice $amount due, provider name and what it is for, client's name.
- [ ] Lost wages forms have been received. If there are no lost wages, there is a note saying so in the medicals section.
- [ ] Examples of pain and suffering have been added to the pain and suffering bucket.
- [ ] The client has confirmed all medical providers, dates of service, lost wages, and pain and suffering examples, and they are noted in the file. If the client confirmed by email, the email is saved in the file. **Written confirmation from the client about the providers is needed.**
- [ ] ALL medical records and itemized bills have been received.
- [ ] ALL balances have been verified for each provider the client treated with. If there was a funding company or a lien, it must be noted. The following is required and is part of the balance verification: original charges, payments made and by whom, adjustments made, current balance due. **"Health insurance" is not sufficient: we must know exactly which insurance company paid** (for example Aetna, Peach State, Medicare). If we do not have this information, the balances have not been verified. Document it in the "Balance Verification / Reductions" bucket, and put a note in the memo under the specific provider whose balance you verified.
  - *Example:* #balanceverification Called (provider name), phone number you called, name of the person you spoke with, details of the conversation.
- [ ] All documents saved and named in the case system are also in the shared folder.

### Pre-demand order

Put the pre-demand together in this order:
1. Pre-demand checklist
2. Police report
3. Pictures
4. Itemized bills, in order of treatment
5. Medical records, in order of treatment, including disability slips
6. Lost wages

When all of the above is complete, scan the entire pre-demand, create a task and give it to the case manager to draft the demand. The phase should now be changed to Pre-Demand. Once the case manager drafts the demand, it goes to the attorney for review and approval.

*There are always exceptions. If there is a special circumstance where we can or should send the demand without having all bills and records, bring it to the supervisor's or attorney's attention. We are able to supplement if necessary.*
