### Overview

The purpose of this procedure is to make sure every welcome call is completed correctly.

### General guidelines

- A welcome call must be completed as soon as the team (legal assistant, case manager and attorney) is notified of the new case, and **within 24 hours** after being notified. The team is notified of a new case through the Teams PNC chat, a task and an email.
- In the **Project Vitals** section, add **#Spanishspeaker** or **#Englishspeaker**. Add **#hospital** if the client went to the hospital.
- **Notes:** welcome call notes go in the **Onboarding** bucket.
- The welcome call task is checked off by the attorney and the case manager or legal assistant once the call is complete.
- If the legal assistant completes the call, the case manager reviews the case and lets the legal assistant know if specific information is needed. The case manager reviews the severity of the injuries and whether the client went to the ER.

### The contact card

**Contact Info tab.** The client's full legal name, date of birth, mailing address (including the apartment number if applicable), phone number and email. A copy of the ID needs to be saved to the contact card.
- The contact type is completed as "client". If the client was a passenger, add that next to the client's name. If the client has had several accidents we represent them for, add the date of the accident at the top next to the client's name.

**Details tab.** Gender, marital status, driver's license number, the language the client speaks, and the client's SSN.
- If the client does not have an SSN or refuses to give it, enter 000-000-0000. Also write a note in the "Notes" section, and the client must sign the CMS form or the affidavit that they are not Medicare or Medicaid eligible.

**Client Journey tab.** Check the "Address verified" box once the address has been verified, and enter the date it was verified. If there are companions on the case, we need their names and their relationship to the client.

### What to confirm and collect on the call

**Emergency contact.** Every case should have an emergency contact. Also get written confirmation if the client authorizes us to disclose detailed information to anyone, including their spouse or emergency contact. The emergency contact is in the PI Intake tab under **Personal Info**.

**Obtain missing information.** Intake asks the client for important case information. If something has not been received, follow up on it. Documents needed:
- ID or driver's license
- police report number or the police report
- front and back of the health insurance card
- pictures of any visible injuries
- the estimate of the property damage
- pictures of the client's and the defendant's property damage
- any discharge documents from places where the client has already treated
- emergency contact information, in case we cannot reach the client

**Insurance.** Ask whether the client has personal insurance, or any other car insurance that may not be listed on the police report. Ask whether they have a **resident relative** and explain what a resident relative is.

**Injuries.** Confirm all injuries and update them in the PI Intake tab under **Injuries**. Think from head to toe. It is extremely important to list every injury.
- Screen the client for a possible **TBI**: any sensitivity to light or noise, dizziness, headaches, nausea, mood swings, or being easily irritated? If so, refer them to a neurologist for a proper diagnosis.
- It is important that the client does not see only a chiropractor and does get seen by an MD. If the injuries are severe, do not send the client to a chiropractor.

**Prior injuries.** Does the client have a prior injury that could have been aggravated by the new accident? Ask whether they have had prior claims and injuries. If so, we need to know when they were injured, how severe the prior injuries were, and whether they hired an attorney. If they did, do they remember whom they hired and how much they received? Depending on the answers, we may need to obtain the file from the prior accident. Complete the **Priors** section (prior injuries, prior lawsuits with an attorney, prior claims, prior bankruptcy).

**Bankruptcy.** Has the client filed for bankruptcy? If so, has it been discharged or is it still active? If it has not been discharged, we need to know whether it was Chapter 7 or 13, and we need the bankruptcy attorney's contact information. Let the attorney or supervisor know about an active bankruptcy case. If the bankruptcy is closed or discharged, write a note; no further action is needed.

**Lost wages.** Has the client lost wages because of the injuries? Send the lost wages (LW) form to the client by email and add it to the meds tab. Add the lost wages information in the Meds tab. If the client says they did not lose wages and will not, add that note in the lost wages section. Complete the **Economic Damages** section of the PI Intake tab (employment status, notes, concurrent employment, tax returns).

**Social media.** Remind the client that what they post online, or are tagged in, may be used against them. Complete the information in the PI Intake tab under **Wrap-Up** (reviewed the social media letter or policy, concerns raised, all social media platforms and user IDs).

### Reminders for the client

- Explain the process and what to expect. Confirm the client has received the legal assistant's and the attorney's contact information, and the PI intro video.
- They should discuss only things related to the property damage with the adjuster. The client should **not give a recorded statement** without speaking with the attorney or legal assistant.
- Be careful with what they post, discuss or are tagged in on social media.
- Keep track of, and give us, examples of non-economic damages (pain and suffering).
- Remind them again how important it is to treat consistently with no gaps in treatment. Confirm the client has been scheduled for treatment.

### After the call

Go to the **Case Summary** tab and enter the date the call was completed. The tab has a section for the attorney and for the person who completed the welcome call (the case manager or the legal assistant). The client contact dates are: attorney initial 24-hour call, legal assistant initial 24-hour call, case manager initial 24-hour call, most recent client contact, and most recent attempted contact.
