Treatment means that we have **confirmed all limits**, so we know what the case may be worth. The client is actively treating, we requested the hospital records (if the client treated at the hospital), and we keep constant contact with the client. Both the liability and the UM dec pages need to be received and documented by the time the case is in Treatment. The hospital records can be requested earlier if we have confirmed at least that liability was accepted, or that UM is available and the police report shows a rear-end collision or clear liability.

**Reminders**
- Add your notes in the **Case Review** bucket.
- Assign tasks outside of the buckets to the team members.
- Use your timer any time you work on a case.
- Check the shared folder to confirm the documents (dec pages, pictures, medical records) are saved both in the document bucket and in the case system.

### Things to review

**Insurance (liability, UM and health insurance)**
- Have the dec pages been received? Are they saved in the correct insurance section? There is a box for when the LOR was sent and when the dec pages were received. These boxes need to be completed.
- Have we confirmed that liability was accepted in the liability section? (The box needs to be completed as "liability accepted". This applies to liability only, not UM.)
- Are the limits entered? (Limits are entered only once we have the dec pages.)
- In the UM section, have we received the UM signed selection form? (The field for whether the UM is add-on or reduced should be completed.)
- Has med pay been confirmed?

**Meds**
- Has an MRI been done? (It needs to be done by week 4.)
- Have the hospital records, ER physician and radiologist been requested? The boxes next to each provider are checked when requested, and the request is saved under each specific provider.
- Is there a lost wages provider? Every case should have one. If the client did not lose wages, there should be a note saying no lost wages.

**General info**
- Has there been recent contact (within 2 weeks)?
- Is there a **#hospital** tag if the client treated at the hospital?
- Is there a **#MP** or **#NoMP** tag?
- Is there a police report? If there is none because the police were not called, there should be a **#nopolicereport** tag.
- Is the **statute of limitations** correct? Review the police report; for an automobile accident it should be 2 years from the date of the accident (check the state's rule).

**Case manager monthly review**
- Has the case manager reviewed the file this month? Case managers review files once a month to make sure cases are worked up correctly. There should be notes in the Case Review bucket from the case manager's review.

**Teams**
- Check the Teams section to confirm the legal assistant and case manager have been entered correctly. If not, they will not get the correct tasks. There needs to be a **green ribbon** next to the legal assistant's name, and both the legal assistant and the case manager should be set as **primary**.
- Are there pending call-back tasks? If so, how long, and has there been contact?

**Phase tracker**
- Has the phase tracker been updated with the date each phase changed and the correct person? Investigation, File Set-Up and Treatment should all be completed by the time the case is in the Treatment phase.
