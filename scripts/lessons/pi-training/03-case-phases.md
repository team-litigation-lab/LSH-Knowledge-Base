### Overview

This procedure explains what each case phase means, so that every case is in the correct phase. Any time a case phase is changed in any project, the **Phase Tracker** must also be updated with the name of the team member who changed the phase and the date it was changed.

### Personal injury case phases

**Lead docket.** A case should not be in this phase. Once a case is transferred from the lead docket it is automatically put in this phase, and it must be changed to Investigation.

**Investigation.** A new case. The welcome call must be completed within 24 hours (see the *Welcome Call Procedure*). Follow up on this phase daily so the case moves to the next phase as soon as possible.
- We still need the police report and/or the insurance information (liability and UM) to file the claims and send out LORs right away.
- We also need the front and back of the health insurance card, to set up the subrogation claim, and a copy of the client's ID.
- Request the hospital discharge documents and the property damage pictures and/or PD estimate.
- This phase means the LORs have not been sent. A case should not be in this phase for longer than a week. The client should be set up for treatment, and we should have confirmed they are actively treating. **(1 week maximum)**

**File Set-Up.** LORs have been sent to liability, UM and health insurance.
- The boxes in the insurance section should be checked with the date the LORs were mailed. Copies of the LORs are saved in the correct provider section.
- File Set-Up means we are waiting for the declaration pages, confirming coverage and that liability has been accepted.
- We need BOTH the liability and the UM dec pages to move to the next phase. We can get verbal limits, but limits should not be entered, and the phase should not change, until we have the declaration pages.
- Follow up on the dec pages weekly. **(30 days; no more than 60 days)**

**Treatment.** The client is actively treating. Both declaration pages are saved, the limits are documented (liability and UM), and it is confirmed that the client is actively treating.
- The correct limits are entered in the insurance tab and confirmed by the case manager.
- Request the ER hospital records as soon as coverage and limits are confirmed. Do not wait until the client is done treating to request the hospital records and bills.
- We have also received correspondence from the health insurance.
- In this phase, contact the client every two weeks to check on them and their treatment status (see the *2-Week Call Procedure*). **(Varies depending on the injuries)**

**Med Recs / Bills.** The client has completed treatment. The client should confirm, preferably in writing by email or text, all the providers they treated with, so none is missed.
- We have requested records and itemized bills and are waiting for them.
- We have requested the updated health insurance explanation of benefits.
- Confirm lost wages and any examples of pain and suffering.
- Once the itemized bills are in, verify the balance with each provider (see the notes on how to verify balances). **(30 to 60 days from the day the client was released)**

**Pre-Demand.** Balances have been verified.
- The legal assistant called the adjuster and confirmed the adjuster's current mailing address where the demand should be mailed.
- We have the updated health insurance explanation of benefits and reviewed it to confirm we have all the providers the client treated with.
- We submitted the bills to the med pay adjuster and have the MPC EOB showing that med pay has been exhausted.
- We have all records, itemized bills, pictures and dec pages (see the *Pre-Demand Requirements checklist*).
- The legal assistant has completed and uploaded the pre-demand in the Demand/Negotiations bucket and tasked the case manager so the demand can be drafted. The attorney should be mentioned in the task.
- The order a case is prepared for demand: police report, pictures, bills, records, lost wages documentation.
- The declaration pages should also be saved in the insurance tab for the case manager to double-check before drafting the demand.
- The case manager or attorney also calls the client to get authorization to send the demand and to verify the providers again. The client is called with an update once the demand is mailed. **(1 week)**

**Pending Companion.** The client is done treating and the case is ready for a demand, but we are waiting for the companion case to draft and mail the demand. **(Varies case by case)**

**Returned to Treatment.** The client returned to treatment after the demand was sent.

**Liability Demand Sent.** The demand has been mailed. Track when it was delivered and set a calendar reminder for the case manager and attorney for 30 days after delivery to the adjuster. The negotiations tab is completed with the demand information, including the certified tracking information.

**Reductions / Pre-UM Demand.** Liability has tendered the policy limits, and the liability settlement is enough to pay the medicals. We can disburse on liability, and there is also UM exposure. We are working on reductions based on the amount received from liability. UM is available, so a UM demand will be sent.

**UM Demand Sent.** Track the demand and set a calendar reminder for the case manager and attorney for 60 days after the demand was delivered to the adjuster. The client needs to be called and updated.

**Negotiation.** The demand has been received and the adjuster has made an offer. The offer is documented in the negotiations tab. **(45 days from the first offer)**

**UM Negotiations.** The UM demand was sent and we are negotiating. **(45 days from the first offer)**

**Reductions.** We have received a final offer and the case has settled. We are working on the reductions to get the final case amount and the final reduction approval.
- Note every reduction request and approval on each provider.
- Send reductions as soon as possible to avoid delays. **(30 days)**

**Need Check.** The case has settled, the reductions are done, and the client has signed the exit letter and release. We are only waiting for the settlement check so we can disburse. The case manager and attorney follow up with the adjuster on the status of the settlement check.

**Accounting.** Exit packets are completed and given to the accounting department to write the checks.

**Disbursement.** Checks have been received from accounting and are being disbursed.

**Closing.** All checks have been mailed to the providers, and the settlement check was given or mailed to the client. Accounting monitors that all the checks have cleared. Once all have cleared, the case is moved to Archive.

**Litigation Review.** The case is being reviewed for possible litigation.

**Pre-Suit.** We are waiting to file suit, either because the suit is being drafted or because it has been drafted and is waiting for attorney review and approval. **(30 days)**

**Pending Service.** We filed suit and are waiting for the defendant(s) to be served. **(15 days; calendar it if not completed within 15 days with the process server)**

**Answer.** The defendant(s) have been served and we are waiting for them to file an answer. **(45 days from the date of service; it is calendared)**

**Discovery.** We are exchanging discovery and depositions. **(6 months from the defendant's answer)**

**Pending Trial.** Discovery is over and we are waiting for trial. **(Depends on the court; could be 6 months to a year or longer)**

**Worker's Comp.** A workers' compensation case. The name of the attorney it was referred to is added as a hashtag and in the case summary tab.

**Wrongful Death.** Not really used. Add the **#wrongfuldeath** hashtag to wrongful death cases and use the phases listed above that apply, for example File Set-Up, Demand Sent or Negotiations.

**Referred Out.** The case was referred to another attorney.

**Archived.** The case has been settled or closed and all checks have cleared.

### Time allowed for each phase

| Phase | Time allowed |
|---|---|
| Investigation | 1 week maximum |
| File Set-Up | 60 days |
| Medical Records and Bills | 60 days |
| Pre-Demand | 15 days |
| Liability Demand Sent | Over 45 days |
| Negotiations | 30 days |
| Reductions | 30 days |
| UM Demand Sent | Over 75 days |
| Disbursement | Over 30 days |
| Need Check | 30 days |
| Treatment | Varies with the severity of the injuries and the treatment (the 2-week contact calls are reviewed) |
