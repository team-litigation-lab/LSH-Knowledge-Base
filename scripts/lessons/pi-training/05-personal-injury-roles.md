### Virtual Assistant

- File claims and generate LORs (liability and UM).
- Generate subrogation LORs.
- Request hospital medical records and bills and follow up until they arrive. It is the legal assistant's and case manager's responsibility to make sure the specific hospital was given to the VA. Task every two weeks.
- Verify balances.
- Call adjusters to verify the current adjuster contact information for the pre-demand.
- Follow up on the status of dec pages and get verbal limits.
- Name documents on file.
- Complete the tasks assigned to the team.
- Translate.

### Legal Assistant

**Phases: Investigation to Pre-Demand.**
- Complete welcome calls.
- Name documents on file.
- Obtain the police report and assign a task to the VA to file claims. Cases should not be in the Investigation phase for longer than a week.
- Mail out LORs once they are generated. Follow up on dec pages.
- Make the 2-week calls.
- Keep the medical providers updated in the meds tab.
- Get the updated health insurance EOB.
- Update the dates of service once the bills and records are received.
- **Update the treatment status.**
- Complete the health insurance questionnaire and return it to the analyst, or call.
- Follow up with the client for missing information (ID, health insurance, hospital discharge documents).
- Provide updates to providers. Confirm that the client gets an MRI within the first month of the case.
- Send med pay demands.
- **Pay invoices and confirm they are not duplicates.** Document all invoices and expenses in the billable expenses and save them in the document bucket.
- Task the case manager to review the dec pages, and enter the limits once the case manager has reviewed the dec page.
- Send HIPAA authorizations to the providers the firm works with, so medical records and bills can be obtained.
- Bring any issues to the case manager's attention right away.
- Follow up on medical records and bills.
- Make sure cases are in the correct phase.
- Task virtual assistants with miscellaneous tasks.
- Complete the pre-demand package and task the case manager once it is complete for review.
- Mail disbursement checks and demands.
- Complete tasks assigned by the case manager.
- **Task the case manager before changing a case from Treatment to Med Recs and Bills.**

### Case Manager

**Phases: Investigation to Med Recs.** Oversee that the case is being built up and help the legal assistant with anything needed.
**Phases: Pre-Demand to Disbursement.**

- Review all new cases within 24 hours and assign notes for the legal assistant about the welcome call. Identify high-value cases, or cases that can settle quickly (severity of injuries, hospital treatment for days, broken bones).
- Review that the case was set up correctly, by putting all parties on notice right away (UM primary, secondary, defendant) and sending an open records request when needed.
- Review spoliation letters before the attorney reviews them.
- Review the providers where the client is treating. Confirm the medical providers are listed correctly in the meds tab. Confirm that the client is scheduled for an MRI in the first month and is seeing an MD. Build up the case.
- Review the cases in Investigation once a week to make sure we have the information needed to send the LOR.
- Review invoices to confirm they are not duplicates. Contact the records vendor or the providers to cancel duplicate invoices.
- Name documents on file.
- Talk to clients before a case changes from Treatment to Medical Records and Bills, to make sure the client is done treating (check treatment, policy limits, property damage).
- Bring issues to the attorneys' attention after the case manager has tried to resolve them first.
- Verify that the welcome call was made, and give the legal assistant information to ask clients within 24 hours after the welcome call was assigned. Add notes when cases are reviewed in the Onboarding bucket (see the onboarding call procedure).
- Check cases in the Treatment phase at least once a month, to make sure the clients are being called and the case is being worked correctly.
- Complete VIP welcome calls, and review the file before the legal assistant makes the welcome call, to find any issues or specific questions the legal assistant should ask.
- Make sure cases are in the correct phase.
- Review the pre-demand to make sure the legal assistant completed all steps: med pay exhausted, balances verified, providers verified, adjuster contact information verified, and all records and bills on file.
- Draft demands.
- Send releases to adjusters and save them in the exit bucket.
- Help the attorneys with lien searches. Attorneys should also check for liens, and all searches should be saved in the case management system and the document bucket.
- Help the attorney with negotiations (follow up on checks and releases).
- Complete reductions right away.
- Complete the case summary and the final settlement information in the case management system.
- Complete exit letters, and keep track of the numbers (settlements, demands written, cases settled).
- Do a lien search, or confirm that the attorneys completed the lien search, before disbursement.
- Update the PI board.

### Meetings

- The supervisor meets with the team by the **17th of every month** to go over cases and see whether the team is on track to meet its goal.
- The legal assistant and case manager meet briefly **once a week** to plan the week.
- The case manager and attorney meet **once a month** to go over cases, focusing on VIP cases and reviewing statute of limitations cases.

### Notes

- During the welcome call, the team adds the **#hospital** hashtag, or enters a note in the Treatment bucket saying that the client did not treat at the hospital.
- **Case summary:** the settlement summary is completed within **60 days**, with the projected settlement date and the projected settlement goal.
