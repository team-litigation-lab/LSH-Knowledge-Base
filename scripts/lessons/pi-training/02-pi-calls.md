Use the case phases (see *Case Phases Procedure*) to know who a call should be transferred to, and who should be tasked and mentioned in the chat.

- The **legal assistants** are primary on cases in the initial phases, through Pre-Demand.
- The **case manager** is primary for calls from after the demand is sent until disbursement.
- If the team is not available, assign the task to the primary team member and write down the **name of the person who called, where they called from and their phone number**, so the call can be returned. Often, when a call goes to voicemail, the caller does not leave a number.

### PNC calls (potential new clients)

PNC calls are a priority and must be answered **immediately**. A PNC should never have to wait; a team member needs to respond. The intake coordinator is primary, but the whole team helps if the intake coordinator is not available.

Screening a PNC call, get:
- the full legal name (first, middle, last, and second last name if applicable)
- the referral source (how they heard about the firm)
- the type of accident (for example motor vehicle accident or workers' compensation)
- the preferred language (English or Spanish)
- a phone number

Post this information in the PI PNC chat. If there is no immediate response, include **@everyone**.

### Other important calls

The other important calls are clients calling, and adjusters calling when the case is in negotiations. Collection companies and other providers are not urgent most of the time.

### Who is primary?

**Legal assistant and case managers** are mentioned in Teams when the case is in one of these phases:
- Investigation
- File Set-Up
- Treatment
- Med Recs and Bills
- Pending Companion
- Pre-Demand
- Disbursements

**Case manager and attorney** are mentioned when the case is in one of these phases. There is a separate chat for cases in negotiations, since the attorneys are not in the PI incoming call chat.
- Liability Demand Sent
- Negotiations
- UM Negotiations
- UM Demand Sent
- Reductions

If a client is calling and a call-back task is more than **3 days overdue**, message the attorney or supervisor.

For cases in **Referred Out** or **Probate**, transfer the caller to the team member who works on referred-out and probate cases.

### Call process

1. When someone calls, add the information to the Teams chat and mention the legal team.
2. If there is no immediate response from the legal team, transfer the call to the appropriate team member and add a task for that team member.
3. If a team member does respond and asks for the call to be transferred, add a note that the call was transferred.
4. In every task, include the name of who called, where they called from and a call-back number.
