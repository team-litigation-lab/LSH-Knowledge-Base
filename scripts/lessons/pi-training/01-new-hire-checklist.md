A checklist for a new member of the personal injury team. The supervisor goes through each document with the new hire and ticks it off.

### Documents to hand over and discuss

- PI Calls
- Case Phases Procedure
- Filevine Tasks Procedure
- Personal Injury Roles
- Naming Documents
- Welcome Call Procedure
- Documenting on Filevine and general information to remember
- 2-Week Call and Balance Verifications
- Treatment Case Review
- Pre-Demand Requirements
- Procedure on how to process invoices
- Withdraw and Terminated

### Things to discuss

- **How to document notes on Filevine.** Keep cases updated, in the correct phase, and well noted.
- **Calls.** The firm's process for incoming and outgoing calls. All calls must be returned within 24 business hours.
- **Teams.** Microsoft Teams must be on at all times and kept updated if team members are not available.
- **Tasks.** Stay up to date with tasks.
- **Updates.** Updates are sent through Teams and email. When an update is sent, team members must acknowledge that they read and understood it. If there are questions, tell the supervisor and ask for clarification. The firm is growing fast, so keeping up with changes is very important.
- **Emails.** The personal injury email and the personal injury internal email.

### Tips to be successful

- Time management and prioritizing are essential to succeed in the role.
- Run a report **every 2 weeks** (mandatory). Filter the cases by last contact and by phase.
- Check voicemails, emails and tasks daily so they do not pile up.
- Enter notes in Filevine while you are on the phone, to avoid doing the work twice.
- Know your numbers and expectations. Starting numbers are sent out monthly.
- Keep tasks to a minimum, and use the calendar in Filevine to keep track of important dates.
