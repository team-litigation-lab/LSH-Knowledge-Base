Ody Process is the process server the firm uses to serve defendants. When the firm asks Ody for the invoice or the return of service on an old job, Ody answers that both are available much faster on its website. Use the steps below instead of emailing for them.

### Find a return of service or invoice on Ody's website

1. Go to **odyprocess.com**.
2. Click **Web Tracking**.
3. Scroll down to the **red box** to avoid the cookies notice.
4. Sign in with the firm's Ody username and password. These are not stored here. Ask your supervisor or the litigation point of contact for them.
5. You are now on the **Dashboard**. You can sort the jobs by name, case and so on.
6. Click the **blue numbers** (Ody's job number) to see every detail about the service.
7. At the bottom of the page you will see the **return of service** and the **invoice**, if the job is closed.

### Tips

- Check the dashboard first for any return of service or invoice on an old job, before you email Ody.
- File the return of service in the case file, and give the invoice to the paralegal or accountant who handles process server payments.
- If a job is not closed, the return and the invoice will not be there yet. Follow up on the return of service as described in *Litigation Procedure 2: Service of Pleadings*.
